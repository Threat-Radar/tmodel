## Table 7: applicability ratings (2026-10-19)

The five-axis ratings that [dimensions.md](dimensions.md) asks for in its Table 7: one row for every row of [Table 9](#table-9-library-records-after-phase-2-2026-10-18), in the same order and groups. The `#` column numbers Table 9's rows from 1 to 121, and a cell that says "row N" means this column.

The rubric is RPT-0005's, so ratings can be compared across the two reports: 0 = absent, 1 = mentioned or weak, 2 = partial (needs extension), 3 = strong, each within the source's own scope. Each axis cell gives the rating, the field or section that justifies it, and a locator. A locator such as `fanout-tools §1.5` is section 1.5 of [fanout-tools.md](fanout-tools.md); the others are sections of the source itself. `?` means not read, with the reason in the cell. `n/a` marks a row that is not a source of claims: a test input, or a view of a record that another row rates.

**How the rows were made.** On 2026-10-19 five rating agents (Claude Code) each took one batch of rows: identity and naming, formats and baselines, hardware and firmware, the bridge to vulnerability data, and tools and studies. Where a Phase 1 fan-out had rated a source (section 4 of each fan-out file), the agent checked that rating against the fan-out's evidence and carried it over: 56 rows, a few with a value changed and the reason given in the `basis` column. Where two fan-outs, or RPT-0005, rated the same source differently, the agent chose one value per axis: 21 rows, each listed under [Disagreements settled](#disagreements-settled). The 40 sources nobody had rated were rated from their own text, fetched again where needed, and 4 rows are `n/a`. These ratings are AI-produced and have not been reviewed by a human.

**Conventions applied across the batches.**

- A paper that defines no record format rates 0 on the four record axes, and 2 on AI-grounding when it has a stable id and open text, as fanout-identity rates SBOMproof. A paper that defines a format of its own (rows 24 and 30) is rated on that format.
- Regulations, guidance and baselines are rated on what they ask SBOM data to carry. A guide that explains a format (rows 40 and 47) is rated on the content it explains, with Object model capped at 2.
- A row that covers two or more documents shows the higher value on each axis; the cell names the document it comes from and gives the other's value.
- On Human review, a signature alone rates 0, because it records origin, not a decision. An automated appraisal or approval result, a registry's stated rule for admitting entries, and a catalog's recorded outcome per entry each rate 1.
- Paywalled texts are rated from public material only (contents lists, scope statements and documents that quote them), so their ratings are lower bounds, and their AI-grounding is 1.
- Row 1's AI-grounding 3 rests on the full extraction (FX-1) of ECMA-427 in Threat-Radar/library#24, which is still open; without it, the fan-out's 2 stands. Rows 8 and 68 also cite the extractions in Threat-Radar/library#28 and #29, which are open too, but their values do not depend on them.
