Overview

A measurement study on image classifiers, not GenAI applications. Building a classifier on a clean encoder pre-trained on public data (OpenAI's CLIP) makes certified defenses — against data poisoning and backdoors (bagging, KNN), adversarial examples (randomized smoothing) and privacy attacks (differential privacy, exact unlearning) — both more accurate and stronger (§1). Example on STL10: bagging accuracy rises from 0.352 to 0.979 and the certified poisoning size from 1.3 to 68.8; differentially private accuracy rises from 0.237 to 0.956 (§1).
