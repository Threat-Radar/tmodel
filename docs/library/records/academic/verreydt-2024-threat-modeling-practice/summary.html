Overview

Semi-structured interviews on how large Dutch organizations practise threat modeling: 13 participants from 7 organizations (each with thousands of employees), in 10 interviews. Threat modeling is valued both for finding threats and for raising developers' security awareness, and motivating developers matters more than enforcing it as compliance. The challenges named are managing scope, getting architectural documentation, scaling, and following up on results. Participants see the opportunity for tooling such as CI/CD integration to trigger reassessment when a change may introduce new threats, but none of the organizations do so, "mostly due to the lack of tool support", so threat modeling "remains mostly a one-time activity" and models are rarely updated.
