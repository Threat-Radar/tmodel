Overview

A replication of an earlier controlled experiment comparing two variants of STRIDE: per element (threats enumerated for each DFD element) and per interaction (threats enumerated for each interaction between elements). 45 student participants in teams, with about two hours for the analysis, measured on productivity (correct threats per hour) and precision. In the preprint's tables, per-element teams averaged 2.5 correct threats per hour at 0.40 precision, and per-interaction teams 3.57 at 0.43. Per-interaction teams "performed better (albeit, not significantly)", contradicting the original study's claim that this variant suits expert analysts better. Both groups reported many false positives.
