Overview

DSOMM grades DevSecOps practice in 251 activities across 6 dimensions (Agentic AI; Build and Deployment; Culture and Organization; Implementation; Information Gathering; Test and Verification) and 24 sub-dimensions, each at a level 1–5 ("Basic understanding" … "Advanced deployment of security practices at scale"). Each activity states a risk and a measure, often an assessment ("Show …"), difficulty (knowledge/time/resources), usefulness, dependencies, implementing tools, and references to OWASP SAMM v2, ISO 27001 (2017, 2022), OpenCRE and (12) D3FEND. Its application tracks implementation and evidence per team.
