Overview

The NSF solicitation that funded the Prototype Open Knowledge Network (Proto-OKN) — a build (not pure research) effort to stand up an integrated, federated data-and-knowledge infrastructure. Three themes: (1) use-case knowledge graphs built with federal agencies to address societal challenges; (2) the "fabric" — common cloud-based technical infrastructure to interconnect the graphs into one federated network; (3) education & outreach. Multi-agency (NSF lead; NIH, NASA, NIJ, NOAA, USGS partners; DARPA/DOT/Army/VA interest). ~$20M, ~10–13 awards, cooperative agreements, tightly-coordinated cohort.

Hard requirements: real federal-agency engagement and multi-source data integration (not single-source); detailed method (tools, workflows, ontology, KG construction); committed end users + data providers; quarterly deliverables with milestones — alpha at 12 mo, beta at 24 mo, prototype deployment at 30 mo; data-ethics/governance taken seriously.

Relevance to tmodel

A blueprint for a federated KG built for a purpose, with governance and provenance, run as a cohort with a shared fabric — the same shape tmodel's threat knowledge graph aspires to. The milestone/deliverable discipline and the emphasis on real users + agencies mirror our compliance/audit and human-review goals. tmodel could later align with, or federate into, this network.
