Overview

A stub: not read in full. Report of the Auto-ISAC SBOM Working Group on SBOM background and effective practices in the automotive industry, from its 2023 and 2024 proceedings. It surveys SBOM work in government, industry and standards, explains what makes the automotive supply chain different, and covers pre-sale considerations (contracts, formats, vulnerability exchange), SBOMs in development, and exchanging and collaborating with SBOMs across the supply chain. Cited by tmodel RPT-0004 (tmodel#38): automotive SBOM practice: recommended formats, responsibilities across OEM and supplier tiers, and the observation that UN R155 does not explicitly refer to SBOMs.
