Overview

ETSI's definition of an AI threat. Its scope (clause 1) says it "defines what an Artificial Intelligence (AI) threat is and defines how it can be distinguished from any non-AI threat", and presents the model "in the form of an ontology to give a view of the relationships between actors representing threats, threat agents, assets and so forth". The ontology extends the threat and threat-agent taxonomy of ETSI TS 102 165-1, and it "applies to AI both as a threat agent and as an attack target" (clause 1, NOTE 1). The outline covers the move from taxonomy to ontology (clause 4), a threat landscape with adversarial goals, attack surface and a trust model (clause 5), and the core SAI ontology relationships (clause 6).
