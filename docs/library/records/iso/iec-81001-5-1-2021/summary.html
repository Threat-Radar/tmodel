Overview

IEC 81001-5-1 is the health-software adaptation of IEC 62443-4-1. Its process requirements (Clauses 4-9) "have been derived from the IEC 62443-4-1 PRODUCT LIFE CYCLE management". They are arranged in the ordering of IEC 62304 so that a medical-software manufacturer can extend its existing 62304 processes rather than build a second lifecycle (0.1).

Conformance can be claimed two ways:

  • full conformance, by implementing Clauses 4-9;
  • for legacy (transitional) health software, by implementing Annex F only (0.3).

Interpretation Sheet 1 (2025) clarifies the risk transfer from manufacturer to operator through a nested classification of software items (MAINTAINED ⊂ SUPPORTED ⊂ REQUIRED). Only maintained items carry the manufacturer's security-update delivery and integrity duties.
