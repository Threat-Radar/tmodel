tmodel · prototype · #14

Attack feasibility vectors

A standalone prototype of the three ISO/SAE 21434 Annex G feasibility methods, used to check layout and behaviour before anything touches the schema, engine, app or real threat models.

Example data. Every threat on this page is invented and the handles are placeholders. This page does not read or change any threat model, and edits made here are never saved. The computed ratings follow ISO/SAE 21434 Annex G values; the window-of-opportunity "not exploitable" level, marked †, is a tmodel extension.

Threat scenarios

Every row starts with the default method, attack potential. A human then either approves it as it stands (● Reviewed as is) or changes the method or values (▲ Changed by human). Hover a chip with an outline to preview its history; click to pin it. Open a row for the expanded view.
